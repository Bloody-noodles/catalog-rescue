Data and privacy

Catalog Rescue runs on Streamlit Community Cloud. Uploaded catalogs are handled by the running app to perform analysis and provide results in your session. This project does not implement a separate catalog database or account system. The hosting provider may process technical data under its own terms and privacy practices.

Do not upload confidential, personal, or otherwise sensitive data unless you have reviewed the hosting provider’s terms and are authorized to use the service for that data. Keep your original file: the app does not replace your local copy.

This summary describes the current app implementation and is not legal advice.
